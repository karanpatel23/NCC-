The NDB-II Package 5 scope covers eight major district roads in Madhya Pradesh:

• Sulgaon–Nimar Kheri–Bangarda–Dhangoan–Kalmukhi (MP-MDR-50-03), 22.08 km.
• Beed–Sheljla–Selda–Sant Singaji Pul–Charkheda–Chanera–Bard–Piplani (MP-MDR-50-07), 30.415 km.
• Khandwa–Ahmadpur–Kalmukhi–Kelwa (MP-MDR-50-15), 52.925 km.
• Jalkuwa–Bhagiyapura–Semalia–Gudi (MP-MDR-50-21), 23.84 km.
• Pandhana–Nihal Badi–Dabhi–West Nimad Border (MP-MDR-50-23), 17.975 km.
• Pandhana–Arud–Borgaon (MP-MDR-50-24), 19.08 km.
• Piploda–Kumtha–Bhilkhedi–Saraya–Chanera–Dongargaon–Borgaon (MP-MDR-50-26), 17.975 km.
• Bhawasa–Malveer–Nai Chondi–Jambupani (MP-MDR-51-17), 14.84 km.
